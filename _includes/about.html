About

I am a fourth-year Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Illinois at Chicago, advised by Prof. Philip S. Yu, and a member of the Big Data and Social Computing Lab. My research interests center on retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), LLM agents, and their applications in recommender systems, though not limited to them.

I am also collaborating with W.W. Grainger, Inc. on retrieval-augmented generation for large-scale industrial product search.

Previously, I earned my M.S. in Computer Science at UT Dallas under Prof. Latifur Khan, working on domain-specific language models, and my M.S./B.S. in Industrial Engineering at Sungkyunkwan University under Prof. Mye Sohn, focusing on recommender systems.

For full details on my research and publications, see my CV.

I am actively seeking Spring 2027 and Summer 2027 research internship positions. Please feel free to reach out at wyang73@uic.edu.
